About个人简介

Dr. Xianping Du is an Associate Professor in the School of Ocean Engineering and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University. His research develops intelligent design methods for complex engineering systems, with a focus on structural and multiphysics optimization and offshore wind energy.杜现平博士现任中山大学海洋工程与技术学院副教授，致力于复杂工程系统智能设计方法研究，重点关注结构与多物理场优化及海上风电。

Before joining Sun Yat-sen University in 2022, he was a postdoctoral research associate at Rutgers University, contributing to a U.S. Department of Energy project on multifidelity design software for large wind turbines. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University in 2019.2022年加入中山大学前，他曾在罗格斯大学从事博士后研究，参与美国能源部大型风机多保真设计软件项目。2019年获Embry-Riddle航空大学机械工程博士学位。

Education Ph.D., Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (2019) · M.S., Hunan University (2015) · B.S., Shandong University of Technology (2012)教育经历 Embry-Riddle航空大学博士（2019）· 湖南大学硕士（2015）· 山东理工大学学士（2012）

Research directions研究方向

01

Floating offshore wind浮式海上风电

02

Structural control and safety结构控制与安全

Structural stability, semi-active control, safety assessment, and monitoring under extreme marine conditions.极端海洋环境下的结构稳定性、半主动控制、安全评估与健康监测。

03

Intelligent engineering design工程智能设计

Data-driven methods, surrogate models, and optimization for complex systems with coupled physics and uncertainty.面向多物理场耦合与不确定性问题的复杂系统数据驱动方法、代理模型与优化。
