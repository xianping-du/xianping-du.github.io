About

Dr. Xianping Du is an Associate Professor in the School of Ocean Engineering and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University. His research develops intelligent design methods for complex engineering systems, with a focus on structural and multiphysics optimization and offshore wind energy.

Before joining Sun Yat-sen University in 2022, he was a postdoctoral research associate at Rutgers University, contributing to a U.S. Department of Energy project on multifidelity design software for large wind turbines. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University in 2019.

Education Ph.D., Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (2019) · M.S., Hunan University (2015) · B.S., Shandong University of Technology (2012)

Research directions

01

Floating offshore wind

Platform dynamics, aeroelastic and control interactions, and control co-design for floating wind turbines.

02

Structural control and safety

Structural stability, semi-active control, safety assessment, and monitoring under extreme marine conditions.

03

Intelligent engineering design

Data-driven methods, surrogate models, and optimization for complex systems with coupled physics and uncertainty.
